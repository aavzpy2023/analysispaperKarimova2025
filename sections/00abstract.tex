\begin{abstract}
	Predictive modeling in low-data bioactivity regimes frequently succumbs to overfitting, silent data leakage, and unwarranted architectural complexity. To isolate the drivers of predictive fidelity, we executed a leak-free benchmarking framework evaluating classical machine learning ensembles against Directed Message Passing Neural Networks (ChemProp D-MPNN) for the inhibition of \textit{Toxoplasma gondii} dihydrofolate reductase (TgDHFR, $N = \NCompounds$). Subjecting four molecular representation spaces to a rigorous \TotalOuterFolds-fold nested cross-validation protocol revealed that explicit inductive bias supersedes algorithmic depth. Classical stacking ensembles trained exclusively on 2D Morgan fingerprints achieved $R^2 = \MorganRTwoMean \pm \MorganRTwoStd$ ($\text{MAE} = \MorganMaeMean$). This lightweight topological approach statistically matched computationally intensive 2D/3D hybrid descriptors ($R^2 = \RdkitTwoDThreeDFpRTwoMean$, $p = \PairedTTestP$) and the best-scoring representation (RDKit 2D descriptors with fingerprints, $R^2 = \RdkitTwoDFpRTwoMean$), and exceeded the D-MPNN ($R^2 = \ChemPropDMPNNFoldRTwoMean \pm \ChemPropDMPNNFoldRTwoStd$, $\text{MAE} = \ChemPropDMPNNFoldMae$) by a margin smaller than the fold-to-fold variability, consistent with data scarcity restricting end-to-end representation learning. Deploying the optimized classical architecture to screen FDA-approved therapeutics prioritized high-potency antifolates and high-efficiency scaffolds, including triamterene (ligand efficiency = 0.49) and pyrimethamine (ligand efficiency = 0.53). Orthogonal validation via AutoDock Vina molecular docking resolved ligand-based screening discordances, filtering out structure-based false positives (e.g., bisacodyl) and confirming high-affinity consensus leads, most notably pralatrexate (docking energy: $-9.50$~kcal/mol). In data-constrained QSAR environments, the fixed structural priors of 2D fingerprints act as a natural regularizer, providing a computationally efficient paradigm for target-specific drug repurposing that makes deep graph representations and 3D conformational overhead unnecessary for this target.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:}
