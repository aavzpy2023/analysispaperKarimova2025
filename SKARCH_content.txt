> **Algorithmic Audit & Optimization of Drug Discovery Frameworks**
>
> An advanced computational pipeline that integrates **Ensemble Machine Learning** (Stacking Regressors), **ADMET Profiling** (TDC), and **Bio-physical Simulations** (AutoDock Vina) to identify potent inhibitors of the *TgDHFR* enzyme. This project challenges and improves upon existing Deep Learning (GNN) baselines by applying the principle of parsimony, robust heuristic filtering, and rigorous validation.

METHODS (summary — copy/adapt directly into the manuscript)
====================================================================================================
- Training dataset: 756 unique compounds (column 'Smiles', target 'pIC50 Value'), matching the size of the original paper's dataset (873 compounds after BindingDB+ChEMBL deduplication).
- Base models compared (6): Random Forest, Extra Trees, LightGBM, XGBoost, SVR (RBF kernel), Partial Least Squares, k-Nearest Neighbors (cosine metric).
- Architectures evaluated per representation: 41 (all single, pairwise, and triple combinations of the 6 base models, with stacking + RidgeCV as the meta-estimator for combos with 2+ members).
- Molecular representations compared (4):
    - morgan: Morgan fingerprints (ECFP4, 2048 bits) — no physicochemical descriptors
    - rdkit2d: 217 RDKit 2D descriptors (analogous to the paper's "2D" model)
    - rdkit2d_fp: RDKit 2D descriptors + Morgan FP (analogous to the paper's "2D/FP" model)
    - rdkit2d3d_fp: 2D + 3D (geometric, via ETKDGv3 embedding + MMFF94s optimization) descriptors + Morgan FP (analogous to the paper's best model, "2D/3D/FP")
- Validation scheme: nested cross-validation. Outer loop: 5-fold repeated 3 times (15 independent outer splits). Inner loop (architecture selection): 5-fold on the training portion of each outer split. The test portion of every outer fold is NEVER used for model selection, avoiding the optimistic bias of reporting the best score among many configurations evaluated on the same held-out set.
- Preprocessing (non-binary representations): median imputation, standardization (StandardScaler), and positive-importance feature selection via SelectFromModel(RandomForestRegressor), all inside a scikit-learn Pipeline refit on every fold (no information leakage across folds).
- random_state=42 for every split, matching the original paper.
